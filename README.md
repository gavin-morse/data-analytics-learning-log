# Data Analytics Learning Log

This repository is a public record of my progress learning data analytics through realistic business problems, hands-on SQL practice, and eventually larger projects.

## How I am learning

I am trying to avoid two extremes:

- spending all of my time passively watching tutorials or memorizing syntax;
- relying on AI to do the thinking for me.

Instead, I use AI as a tutor and practice partner. It can create realistic datasets, explain concepts, help debug syntax, and give me business questions to investigate. I try to decide what should be measured, write the queries myself, interpret the results, and decide what question to ask next.

The goal is to become technically capable while developing the reasoning skills that matter in actual analyst work.
